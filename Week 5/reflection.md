Before using ai, I identified the main stakeholders, scope, functional requirements, non functional requirements and user stories from the SmartCare brief. AI helped me notice some issues I missed, especially that my scope said staff could update appointment information while FR-08 only allowed the appointment status to be updated. It also noticed that my first user story had an incorrect linked requirement. Some of the ai suggestions went beyond what was actually stated in the client brief. For example, it raised questions about appointment overlap, login, permissions, appointment status values and how much appointment history should be stored. These were useful questions, but they were not confirmed requirements. therefore treated them as unverified or open questions instead of assuming they were required. The main requirement i changed after the review was adding Fr-10 for searching for a patient. This was based on the information provided which stated that the staff had difficulty finding patient information. Requirements need evidence because they should be based on what the client actually needs. Without evidence, assumptions can become requirements and the system could include features the client didnt ask for
